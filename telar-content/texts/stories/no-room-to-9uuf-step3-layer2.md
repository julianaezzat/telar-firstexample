---
title: "Go deeper"
---

This survey shows that student space was not only a planning issue in the 1990s. It was also a communication issue. UCSB was trying to gather student opinions, but the very existence of the survey suggests uncertainty about whether campus spaces were working well for students. The survey makes student experience part of the historical record: what students valued, what felt uncomfortable, and whether university spaces actually supported their daily lives. The 1990s space problem was not only about construction or capacity, but about student voice.
