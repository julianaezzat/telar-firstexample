This matters because the library was not the only campus space under pressure. Students needed places to meet, organize, study, and participate in campus life, but UCSB's infrastructure was struggling to keep up. The later library occupation fits into this larger pattern: students were not just asking for one building to stay open longer, they were responding to a campus where usable student space had become harder to access.
