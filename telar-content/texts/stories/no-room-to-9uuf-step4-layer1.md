The Concerned Students group formed in early November of 1992, only a few months before the occupation in March 1993. The group began with a small number of students who were worried about the future of public education in California. By the time of the library occupation, they described themselves as a "coalition working to educate students, take action, and protect future access to affordable public education."

The flyer listed specific demands: keeping the library open 24 hours during Dead Week and Finals Week, extending library hours during the quarter, increasing work-study hours, and improving graduate and undergraduate study space. It also called for open negotiations with students "during and beyond" the Library Action. The students were not only asking for a temporary fix. They wanted an ongoing conversation about access and university responsibility.
