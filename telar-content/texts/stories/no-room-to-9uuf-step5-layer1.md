The bold design of the flyer matters. Students were not presenting library hours as a minor inconvenience or polite request. The language was loud, direct, and public. It suggested that limited access to the library was something the whole campus needed to notice. By using flyers, slogans, and direct demands, students made study space visible as a campus problem rather than something individuals had to quietly deal with on their own.
